# Whole Health Brainstorm

A single-file brainstorm board for Grade 8 STEAM, Lesson 1: Whole Health. It replaces a Jamboard activity that no longer works.

Students brainstorm in three sections:

- What does being healthy mean to you?
- Choices that can help my health
- Choices that can hurt my health

Each section holds as many idea rows as the student wants to add. When they are done, the app compiles their ideas into one clean grouped list that they can print or copy into their portfolio.

Formative only: nothing is scored, nothing is submitted anywhere, and nothing leaves the student's browser. Single HTML file, no external resources, works offline.
